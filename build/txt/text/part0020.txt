About the Author

Deena Weinstein is Professor of Sociology at DePaul University in Chicago, where she has taught a course on the sociology of rock music for almost two decades and has published numerous scholarly works on rock. She is also a rock critic whose reviews of albums and concerts, as well as interviews with musicians, have appeared in a wide variety of publications. Weinstein places her interest in rock music in the context of cultural theory and has published numerous books and articles on modernism and postmodernism.
